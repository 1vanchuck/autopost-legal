Autopost — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Autopost ("the app") is operated by Ivan Antonov, an individual (the "Operator"). The app publishes video files chosen by the Operator to the Operator's own accounts on video platforms, including YouTube, using the platforms' official APIs.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • An OAuth access/refresh token that lets the app upload videos to the channel of the Google account that granted access (scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload).
  • The video files, titles, descriptions and tags that the user chooses to upload.

The app does not read the user's watch history, subscriptions, comments or private videos, and does not collect data about other YouTube users.

How data is stored and used

Tokens and uploaded content are stored only on the Operator's own device or server and are used solely to publish the videos the user has chosen. Data is not sold, rented, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer access your channel. On request to the contact address below, stored tokens and related data will be deleted within 30 days.

Changes

If the app is opened to other users in the future, this policy will be updated before any new user data is collected.
